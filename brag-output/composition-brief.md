## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: "Crisp, modern, high-velocity school ERP launch film showcasing real multi-role operations"
- Interpretation: Fast, precise UI animations, solid color cards, readable hold times, and satisfying sound-designed feedback.
- Angle: From morning roll call to lab chemistry to month-end staff payroll treasury, EduNexus runs the entire school with instant responsiveness.
- Hook: Rapid multi-role switching and live 6-section status in the first 3.5 seconds.
- Outro / punchline: "CAMPUS ERP SO SMOOTH, EVEN THE PRINCIPAL SMILED."
- Avoid: Generic SaaS jargon, template abstract shapes, washed out colors.

## Visual Identity
- Background: `#FFFDF9` (Canvas Light) / `#0A1612` (Command Dark)
- Accent: `#0A4D44` (Imperial Emerald) & `#0E6B5E` (Teal)
- Card Surfaces: `#FFFFFF`, `#F7F3EB`, `#EFF5F3`
- Text: `#1C2A27` & `#F5F0E6`
- Fonts: System UI sans-serif (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Roboto) & Monospace for figures.
